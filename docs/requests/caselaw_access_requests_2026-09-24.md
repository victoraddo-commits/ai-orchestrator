# Juris Kai — Case-Law Access Requests

Prepared 2026-09-24. These are ready-to-send formal requests to unlock **legitimate** access to Ghanaian judgments and the missing Acts. Fill the `[BRACKETED]` fields, then send by email/post.

## Why these, and not scrapers
- **GhaLII / AfricanLII**: `robots` sets `ai-input=no`; terms forbid scraping. **No.**
- **Laws.Africa**: terms require permission (cookies/agents). **No.**
- **WorldLII / CommonLII**: HTTP 403. **No.**
- The **decision text itself is not copyrightable** (Ghana Copyright Act 2005, s.8(1)(b)); enactments likewise (s.8(1)(a)). But the **Judicial Service's e-Judgment system is gated and its site carries "© All Rights Reserved"** — so the clean, lawful route is a **licence** or an **RTI request**, not scraping.

## Contacts (verified from judicial.gov.gh)

- Address: P.O. Box GP 119, Accra (Law Court Complex, Accra)
- Tel: +233 302-663 951 / 663 954 / 666 671
- Website: https://judicial.gov.gh
- It publishes an **RTI Manual**, so it has a designated **RTI Officer** — address the RTI application to "The RTI Officer, Judicial Service of Ghana".

**Council for Law Reporting** — publishes the Ghana Law Reports (GLR); relevant for reported judgments + citations.

**Attorney-General's Department / Legislative Drafting Division** — custodian of the **Revised Edition of the Laws of Ghana** and the official statute text.

## Letter 1 — Licence request to the Judicial Service (e-Judgment / judgments)

```
[YOUR NAME]
[ORGANISATION]
[ADDRESS]
[EMAIL] | [PHONE]
[DATE]

The Judicial Secretary
Judicial Service of Ghana
P.O. Box GP 119
Accra

Dear Sir/Madam,

RE: REQUEST FOR A LICENCE TO ACCESS AND USE GHANAIAN JUDGMENTS FOR A NON-PROFIT LEGAL RESEARCH ASSISTANT

I write on behalf of [ORGANISATION], which operates "Juris Kai", a legal research and educational assistant for Ghanaian law. The system answers questions about Ghanaian law and is used for legal education and research. It runs entirely on our own local infrastructure; it does not publish judgments to the public.

We wish to ensure that our use of your judgments is fully lawful and approved. We therefore respectfully request a written licence (or terms of use) permitting us to:

1. obtain electronic copies of judgments in the e-Judgment system (or an agreed subset), on a one-off and ongoing basis;
2. store those judgments in a private, access-controlled research database;
3. use them to ground answers (retrieval-augmented generation) for our users, with clear attribution to the court and the Judicial Service;
4. reproduce short verbatim quotations from judgments in answers, for accuracy and verifiability.

We understand and accept that:
- the judgments remain subject to any conditions the courts or the Judicial Service impose;
- we will not re-publish the judgments in bulk, sell them, or misrepresent them;
- we will comply with any redaction or privacy conditions you specify, and will not use the material for any purpose you prohibit.

We would also welcome guidance on any fee, form or agreement required.

We are happy to provide any further information and to meet your technical team.

Yours faithfully,

[YOUR NAME]
[TITLE], [ORGANISATION]
[EMAIL] | [PHONE]
```

## Letter 2 — RTI Application (Right to Information Act, 2019 (Act 989))

```
[YOUR NAME]
[ORGANISATION]
[ADDRESS]
[EMAIL] | [PHONE]
[DATE]

The RTI Officer
Judicial Service of Ghana
P.O. Box GP 119
Accra

APPLICATION FOR ACCESS TO INFORMATION UNDER THE RIGHT TO INFORMATION ACT, 2019 (ACT 989)

1. Applicant: [YOUR NAME], of [ORGANISATION], [ADDRESS], [EMAIL], [PHONE].

2. Information requested:
   (a) electronic copies of published judgments of the Superior Courts of Judicature of Ghana (Supreme Court, Court of Appeal, High Court) for the period [YEAR] to date, in a machine-readable format (PDF/text), where held;
   (b) where available, the metadata (court, date, suit number, parties, citation) associated with those judgments;
   (c) any bulk-access arrangement, dataset, or interface the Judicial Service provides for legal-information institutes, researchers, or developers.

3. Purpose: to operate a non-profit legal research and educational assistant for Ghanaian law that answers questions using Ghanaian judgments, with attribution. The information will be used lawfully, will not be published in bulk, and will not be used to misrepresent the courts.

4. Form and manner: preferably electronically (secure download link or storage medium); we will meet the cost of any medium or copying as required.

5. Exemptions: if any part is exempt, please sever and provide the non-exempt part, with the ground of refusal under the Act.

6. I request a written decision within the time provided by the Act. If refused, I ask for the reasons and the internal review/appeal procedure (including to the Right to Information Commission).

Yours faithfully,

[YOUR NAME]
[TITLE], [ORGANISATION]
```

## Letter 4 — Attorney-General (Legislative Drafting) — Revised Edition + missing Acts

```
[DATE]
The Attorney-General and Minister for Justice
Ministry of Justice and Attorney-General's Department
[ADDRESS]

RE: REQUEST FOR THE OFFICIAL REVISED EDITION TEXT OF SPECIFIED ACTS

Dear Sir/Madam,

[ORGANISATION] maintains a private, non-commercial legal-research corpus of Ghanaian legislation for "Juris Kai", a legal research/education assistant. Enactments are excluded from copyright under s.8(1)(a) of the Copyright Act, 2005 (Act 707/690 series). To ensure accuracy, we request the official text of the following (or confirmation of their current titles/status):

- Evidence Act, 1975 (NRCD 323)
- Adoption Act, 1962 (Act 104)
- Alternative Dispute Resolution Act, 2010 (Act 798)
- Foreign Tribunals Evidence Act, 1960 (Act 19)
- Customary Marriage and Divorce (Registration) Law, 1985 (PNDCL 112)

We will attribute the source and not misrepresent the text.

Yours faithfully,
[YOUR NAME]
```
