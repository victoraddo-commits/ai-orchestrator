# RTI Application — corrected to the official requirements

**Status:** My earlier draft was a free-form letter. The RTI Commission's *Information Manual (2022)* requires the **Standard RTI Application Form** (Appendix A) and specific fields. This version complies.

## What the manual actually requires (source: RTI Commission Information Manual 2022, s.3 + Appendix A)
1. **Application must be in writing, using the standard RTI Application Form** (Appendix A) — downloadable/completable on the RTIC or Ministry of Information website.
2. Required fields: **Date of Application; Name of applicant; Name of organisation (if any); contact details (Telephone, Email, Postal Address, Fax); brief description specifying the *type and class* of information *including cover dates*; payment of relevant fee; Signature/thumbprint.**
3. **Provision of identification** — at least one valid ID: Driver's Licence / Passport / National ID / Voter's ID.
4. **State the format and mode of transmission** (certified true copy / normal photocopy / electronic copy; post / email / courier / fax).
5. **Response within 14 days** (s.23); transfer within 10 days (s.20); extension max 7 days.
6. The Manual is for requests **to the RTIC**; the same Act and Form apply to any **public institution**, including the Judicial Service.

> ⚠️ **Action for you:** download the blank **Standard RTI Application Form (Appendix A)** from the RTIC/Ministry of Information site and transcribe the fields below into it (or submit it electronically). The block below mirrors Appendix A exactly.

## Cover email (submit with the completed form)

**To:** [Judicial Service RTI Officer — see their RTI Manual page]
**Subject:** RTI Application under the Right to Information Act, 2019 (Act 989) — request for judgments

Dear RTI Officer,

Please find attached my completed **Standard RTI Application Form (Appendix A)** under the Right to Information Act, 2019 (Act 989), addressed to the Judicial Service of Ghana.

In summary, the request seeks **copies of judgments of the Superior Courts** (class: court judgments; type: electronic full-text judgments + metadata; cover dates: [YEARS]) in **electronic copy**, transmitted by **email or a secure download link**, for a non-profit legal research/education assistant. I confirm the material will not be re-published in bulk or misrepresented, and I will comply with any redaction or privacy conditions you specify.

I have attached proof of identity as required. Kindly acknowledge receipt and advise the applicable fee (s.24), which I will pay.

Yours faithfully,
[FULL NAME]
[TITLE], [ORGANISATION]
[EMAIL] | [PHONE] | [POSTAL ADDRESS]

**Attachments:** (1) completed Standard RTI Application Form; (2) copy of valid ID.

- [ ] Fill Date of Birth, TIN (if applicable), ID type + number.

- [ ] State the fee-payment readiness (s.24).
- [ ] Note the statutory clock: **14 days** for a decision; appeal to the RTI Commission if refused.
